24 MARCH 2025 · ENTRY 001

Before it became us.

Before we had a date to celebrate, there was a time when we were still figuring things out.

We were both waiting for the chance to get a little closer. Not everything happened all at once. There was a process, a little patience, and two people who were slowly finding their way to each other.

Then you were the one who confessed first. I guess that was one of the moments that made things a little clearer between us. Something that had been left unspoken finally had a place to go.

And after that, I was the one who asked if we could make it official. A simple question, but one that eventually gave us a date worth remembering.

24.03.2025

And then, there was us.

That was the day we started calling this something more than just getting closer. The day we decided to give this a name and see where it could take us.

Since then, you've become part of my everyday life in ways I didn't expect. Through messages, random stories, little updates, and conversations that sometimes have no particular point.

We may not get to experience everything side by side. There are days when distance is simply something we have to work around. But somehow, we've found our own way of being present in each other's lives.

I don't think a relationship needs to be perfect to mean something. What matters is having someone willing to understand, communicate, make an effort, and keep choosing to be part of the story.

And I hope we never lose that. I hope we keep learning about each other, growing together, and making room for all the ordinary days ahead.

You confessed first. I asked you to stay. And somehow, we became us.
